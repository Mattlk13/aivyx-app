# Aivyx Brand Guide

## The Candlelit Kitchen

Aivyx follows the **Candlelit Kitchen** design philosophy — warm, personal, and crafted.

Instead of the cold, corporate aesthetic common in AI tools, Aivyx feels like a workspace lit by candlelight: intimate, focused, and distinctly human.

## Design Principles

- **Matte, not glossy** — Chalk-like finishes, subtle textures
- **Warm, not cold** — Deep charcoal backgrounds with warm amber and cream
- **Organic, not rigid** — Rounded corners, asymmetric motifs
- **Crafted, not generic** — A hand-made, indie feel

## The Candle Motif

The Aivyx identity centers on a single elegant candle:
- Cream/ivory body with a warm golden-amber flame
- Soft glow effect radiating from the flame tip
- Set against deep charcoal for high contrast
- Minimal, modern flat design

The candle symbolizes:
- **Warmth** — A personal, human-centered AI experience
- **Privacy** — Light that stays within your space
- **Focus** — Illuminating what matters

## Voice & Tone

- **Tagline**: "Your AI. Your Machine. Your Rules."
- **Pitch**: Privacy-first AI agent platform that runs locally
- **Personality**: Knowledgeable but approachable, technical but not intimidating
- **Avoid**: Corporate jargon, hype language, fear-based marketing
